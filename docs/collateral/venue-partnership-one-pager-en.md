## Master text

**Elegance, after sunset.**
Distinctive evenings, designed with your venue.

YMG Events creates distinctive evenings with Genova venues and hosts, bringing together music, hospitality and a memorable guest experience.

### A partnership that starts with the space

- **Creative concept:** a format and guest journey designed for the venue.
- **Music and entertainment:** coordination of live music, DJs and performers.
- **Hospitality:** an experience consistent with the venue's service and identity.
- **Ticketing:** a proposal to evaluate together with the venue.
- **Promotion:** a plan and materials agreed with the venue.
- **Practical production:** timings, roles and responsibilities defined together.

### From first conversation to decision

1. **Start with the place:** space, capacity, calendar and goals.
2. **Design the evening:** concept, entertainment, hospitality and costs.
3. **Decide together:** clear roles, costs and plan before going ahead.

Every partnership is defined case by case. There is no standard revenue split; sales and attendance are not guaranteed. Costs, ticketing, responsibilities and promotion are agreed before deciding whether to proceed.
