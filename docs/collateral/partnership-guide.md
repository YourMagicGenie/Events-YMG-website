## How v3 was condensed

Every term in the guide comes from v3. No clients, venues, event history, guarantees, commission or revenue-split rates, tax status or deposit percentages were added.

**Kept in full:** all fee bands and their labels, the fit call and advisory terms, the five "agreed before promotion" responsibilities, the no-guarantee and revenue-share/referral-commission disclosures, the series framework, the change-control rule, "what changes the quote", the inquiry checklist, the contact details and the footer business identity.

**Shortened:**
- Service descriptions and bullet points are trimmed to one line each. Detail moved into the shared fee note. For example, "the proposal defines the supplier scope and selection work" is now covered by "the written proposal confirms the fee, tax treatment and included scope".
- Advisory: dropped "The proposal states the planning question, deliverables and any credit toward a later engagement."
- Changes: dropped "Applicable tax and approved expenses are shown explicitly" and "any event-day spending authority set in advance". The guide keeps "within the agreed authority" and the written-agreement rule.
- Founder: dropped the closing clause "YMG Events brings that practical background to professional engagements…".
- Page 1 table of contents and page numbers from the six-page layout.

**Small copy edits (please confirm):**
- "venue led" → "venue-led".
- "These guides cover our professional work" → "These fee guides cover our professional work only".
- The hero eyebrow uses the document's "Your Magic Genie Group / Genova, Italy". The section headings "Built around your audience and your space" and "Clear decisions, coordinated delivery" are new short titles placed above the v3 headings "Venue partnerships" and "Delivery and budgets", which are kept as labels.

## Rendering

```bash
node tools/render-partnership-guide.mjs en
```

This writes the review draft and PNG preview. It fails if any text is clipped, or if content would run into the contact panel or footer.

```bash
node tools/render-partnership-guide.mjs en --final
```

This only works after `"status": "approved"` is set in the JSON. It writes `partnership-guide-en.pdf` and copies it to the published `/downloads/` URL.

DM Sans is embedded from static 400/700 instances in `source/fonts/`, built from the site's own subsets by `tools/build-collateral-fonts.py` (SIL OFL; see `source/fonts/OFL-dmsans.txt`). Chromium otherwise embeds the site's variable DM Sans as Type 3 outlines, which render unhinted in some PDF viewers and doubled the file size. Images are re-encoded as JPEG in the browser.

`test/partnership-guide.test.mjs` checks:
- the fee bands and the commercial limits;
- two A4 pages, embedded TrueType fonts (no Type 3) and extractable text;
- the email and website links;
- the file stays under 400 KB;
- the published URLs keep the one-pagers until the JSON is approved.

The same checks run for the Italian guide.
